Why MukoLend?

In Zimbabwe, hundreds of thousands rely on Mukando savings clubs and informal borrowing. But when arrangements are kept only in WhatsApp screenshots or verbal recollections, disagreements happen.

Trusted Communities

Lending occurs strictly inside verified circles with private invitation codes. Only members you know and trust can view or fund requests.

Operational Workflow

How Community Lending Works on MukoLend

A structured four-step lifecycle from emergency request to fulfilled agreement.

1

Join a Trusted Circle

Establish or join a Mukando savings club using a secure, private invitation code generated by the club administrator.

2

Submit Clear Request

Borrower submits a short-term loan request specifying the exact amount, reason, and proposed repayment due date.

3

Dual Agreement Lock

A fellow member accepts the request. Both parties must separately acknowledge the terms before the loan becomes active.

4

Track & Settle

Borrower logs payments with transaction reference numbers. When the balance hits zero, the loan closes automatically.

Important Simulation Disclosure

Simulated Transactions — No Real Money Transferred

MukoLend is a record-keeping and mutual agreement management tool for community groups. This fintech MVP operates entirely on simulated transaction references. We do not integrate with real mobile money or banking networks, and we do not guarantee loans, repayment, or returns.
